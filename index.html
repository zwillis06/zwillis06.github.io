PERSONAL CHANNEL AUTOMATION

The Future Files

A private tool used by the channel owner to prepare original narrated short videos, archive them in Google Drive, and schedule them on YouTube.

Privacy policy · Terms of use · Connected YouTube channel

What the tool does

A daily GitHub Actions workflow develops a sourced historical story, synthesizes narration, renders a vertical video with text scenes, archives the video and production record in Drive, and requests publication at 6 PM Australia/Brisbane. A later check verifies actual public visibility.

This tool is operated for the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel. It is not a public sign-up service and does not accept other users' credentials on this website.

Google permissions

Drive access is limited to files created by or specifically selected for the app. Separate YouTube authorization allows video uploads and read-only checks of channel identity, processing, and visibility. Google authorization takes place on Google's own permission screens.

Information updated 15 October 2026. This site contains no login form, advertising, or analytics. For support, contact the owner through the connected channel.
